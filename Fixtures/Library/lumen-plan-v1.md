# Project Lumen Plan (version 1)

## Overview
Project Lumen installs motion-sensor lighting control in the Lisbon office. Project lead: Priya Raman.

## Budget
The total budget is EUR 48,000. A contingency reserve of 10 percent is included. Finance approver: Ana Costa.

## Timeline
- Prototype firmware: 30 April 2026
- Pilot on the third floor: May 2026
- Launch: 14 June 2026

## Risks
- Sensor delivery may take up to 6 weeks.
- The data privacy review has no owner yet.
